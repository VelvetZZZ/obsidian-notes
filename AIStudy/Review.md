Q2:监督学习和无监督学习的区别？
<mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">1.Supervised Learning：</mark>
	1」Supervised learning learns from<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;"> labeled data</mark>, where each training example contains an input and the corresponding output.
    --- 输入 + 答案   <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">有标签的数据</mark>
	2」Classification 分类
		输出是离散类别 discrete category （cat/dog）
	3」Regression 回归
		输出是连续数字 continuous value（price）
<mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">2.Unsupervised Learning:</mark>
	1」Unsupervised learning discovers<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;"> hidden patterns</mark> or structures in <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">unlabeled data</mark>. ---- 隐藏结构 - 找规律（用户分群-年轻用户/高消费用户）


<mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">3.Reinforcement Learning:</mark>
	1」Reinforcement learning learns through <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">interaction</mark> with an environment and <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">feedback</mark> from rewards.---- 试错与奖惩机制
